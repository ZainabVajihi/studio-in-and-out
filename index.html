The minds behind Studio In & Out

Abdeali Kachwala & Ar. Sakina Kachwala

United by a shared passion for design, Abdeali and Sakina bring together two distinct perspectives, interior design and architecture, to create spaces that are both expressive and purposeful.

As the co-founders of Studio In & Out, their approach goes beyond aesthetics, focusing on thoughtful spatial planning, refined detailing, and a deep understanding of how people experience spaces.

Abdeali brings a keen eye for materials, detailing, and design expression, while Sakina lends her architectural perspective, balancing creativity with spatial clarity and functionality.

Together, they believe that exceptional design is not defined by extravagance, but by intention, individuality, and the seamless harmony of form and function.

Two perspectives. One vision. A distinctive approach to design.
